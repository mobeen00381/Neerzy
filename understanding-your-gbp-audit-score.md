# Understanding Your Google Business Profile Audit Score

If you've just run [Neerzy's free Google Business Profile audit](https://www.neerzy.com/gmb-audit-tool) and landed on a number out of 100, this page exists to answer the question that number raises: **what does this actually mean, and what do I do about it?**

Your score is built from five weighted categories. This page walks through each one — what it measures, why it's weighted the way it is, and the specific, concrete fixes that move it.

## How the Score Is Built

| Category | Weight | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Completeness | 25% | NAP, hours, categories, description, attributes |
| Reviews & Reputation | 25% | Review count, rating, velocity, response rate |
| Visual Content | 20% | Photo count, recency, diversity, video presence |
| Engagement & Activity | 15% | Posting frequency, Q&A activity, review responses |
| Local SEO Optimization | 15% | Keywords, category accuracy, service areas, NAP consistency |

Completeness and Reviews & Reputation carry the most weight — together, half your score — which is why they're the first two places to look if your number is lower than you'd like.

## Completeness (25%)

This category checks whether the basic structure of your profile is actually filled in: business name, address, phone number, website link, business hours, primary category, a written business description, and your listed attributes and services.

**Why it's weighted this heavily:** an incomplete profile gives Google — and AI systems reading your profile — nothing solid to match against a search. A missing category or an empty description isn't a cosmetic gap; it's a direct relevance signal Google can't use.

**How to fix a low Completeness score:**
- Open Google Business Profile Manager and go field by field — most owners are surprised how many fields were left blank when the profile was first created years ago.
- Write out the full ~750-character business description, naturally including your core services and city.
- Add every applicable attribute (free estimates, licensed, veteran-owned, etc.) and every individual service you offer, not just a general category.

## Reviews & Reputation (25%)

This checks your total review count, average star rating, how recently you've received new reviews (velocity), and how consistently you respond.

**Why it's weighted this heavily:** reviews are both a ranking factor and the single biggest trust signal a customer sees before calling — Google and your customers are, in this case, looking at the same evidence.

**How to fix a low Reviews & Reputation score:**
- Prioritize velocity over volume if you're starting from a low base — a steady trickle of new reviews each month outperforms a single burst followed by silence.
- Respond to every review, especially negative ones — response rate is scored directly, and it's often the easiest sub-metric to fix immediately.

## Visual Content (20%)

This checks total photo count against benchmarks (10+, 50+, and 100+ thresholds), how recently photos were uploaded, and whether you have video content.

**Why it's weighted this heavily:** photo volume and recency are among the clearest, hardest-to-fake signals that a business is actually operating and actively maintained — a profile with fresh weekly photos reads as alive in a way text alone can't replicate.

**How to fix a low Visual Content score:**
- Upload photos from every completed job, not just a curated batch once a year.
- Mix photo types: completed work, before-and-afters, your team, your trucks — diversity is scored, not just quantity.
- Add at least one video if you have none — it's a distinct sub-check most competitors skip entirely.

## Engagement & Activity (15%)

This checks whether your profile is claimed, how often you're posting, whether your Q&A section has activity, and whether you're responding to reviews.

**Why it's weighted this way:** activity is Google's clearest proxy for "is this business currently operating," separate from how good the business's reputation already is.

**How to fix a low Engagement & Activity score:**
- Publish at least one Google Post per week — see [Section 14 of the full guide](/seo-for-plumbers#section-14) for simple post templates that require no marketing background.
- Seed your own Q&A section with the 3–5 questions customers ask most, rather than leaving it empty for a stranger to fill first.

## Local SEO Optimization (15%)

This checks keyword usage in your business name and description, category optimization, defined service areas, NAP consistency across the web, and backlinks from your website to your GBP.

**Why it's weighted this way:** this category is less about any single field and more about *consistency* — the same business information stated identically everywhere is what allows Google to build confidence in your listing.

**How to fix a low Local SEO Optimization score:**
- Run a manual NAP check across your website, Yelp, Angi, and the BBB — inconsistencies here are common and easy to miss.
- Make sure your service areas are explicitly defined, not just implied by your business address.
- Link from your website's contact page directly to your Google Business Profile.

## What to Do With Your Score

1. **Note your lowest-scoring category** — that's where the fastest gains are available, not necessarily the category that sounds most important.
2. **Fix the highest-weighted issues first** within that category, since Completeness and Reviews & Reputation move your overall score the most per fix.
3. **Re-run the audit in 30 days.** Most of these fixes (photos, posts, review responses) show measurable score movement within a month.
