<h2 id="quick-answer">Quick Answer</h2>

More people are asking AI tools — ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, Gemini — for local recommendations instead of typing a search and scrolling. Those tools don't crawl the web fresh for every answer. For local businesses, they lean heavily on the same signals Google Maps already uses: your Google Business Profile data, your reviews, and how active and consistent your online presence is.

A business with a thin, outdated Google profile is far less likely to get recommended by an AI tool than one with a complete profile, regular activity, and real reviews — the same fundamentals that already drive Google Maps rankings.

<h2 id="why-this-matters-for-local-trades">Why this is starting to matter for local trades</h2>

A customer who used to search "plumber near me" on Google now sometimes asks an AI assistant the same question conversationally — "who's a good plumber near me that can come out today?" The AI gives a short, direct answer instead of ten blue links. **If your business isn't part of that answer, you don't just rank lower — you may not appear at all, with no page 2 to fall back on.**

This is early. Search behavior is shifting gradually, not overnight, and Google Maps and traditional search still drive the large majority of local discovery today. But the businesses that show up well in AI answers a year from now will mostly be the ones that already had strong, complete, active Google Business Profiles — not businesses that scrambled to catch up after the fact.

<h2 id="what-influences-ai-visibility">What actually influences AI visibility (as far as this can be verified today)</h2>

AI tools don't have a separate, secret local-business database. For local recommendations, they draw on the same public signals that already exist:

- **A complete, accurate Google Business Profile** — name, address, phone, hours, category, service area, all filled in and consistent.
- **Reviews** — both the volume and how recent they are. A business with 4 reviews from two years ago reads very differently than one with steady reviews every month.
- **Activity** — regular Google Posts and fresh photos signal an active, real business rather than an abandoned listing.
- **Website content that matches your profile** — consistent business name, service area, and services across your site and your profile.

None of this is a guess about any one AI product's exact algorithm. These are the same fundamentals that already drive Google Maps visibility, and they're the most defensible things a business can control today, regardless of which AI tools become more common tomorrow.
