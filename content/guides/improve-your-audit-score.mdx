<p>You&apos;ve run <Link href="/gmb-audit-tool" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Neerzy&apos;s free GBP audit</Link> and you have a number. Now the real question: <strong>what do you actually do next?</strong></p>
        <p>This page is the bridge between your score and a specific plan. It won&apos;t repeat what each score measures — that&apos;s covered in <Link href="/understanding-your-gbp-audit-score" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Understanding Your Audit Score</Link> — this page is purely about sequencing: what to fix first, in what order, and what to realistically expect.</p>

        <h2>Step 1: Read Your Three Scores</h2>

        <SeoDiagram caption="Your report has three scores. Local SEO carries the most weight; AEO and GEO cover whether machines can read and quote your website.">
          <AuditResultDiagram />
        </SeoDiagram>

        <p>Neerzy&apos;s audit reports three scores, each out of 100, each with a fixed weight in your overall total:</p>

        <div className="overflow-x-auto">
          <table>
            <thead>
              <tr><th>Score</th><th>Weight</th><th>What it covers</th></tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
              <tr><td><strong>Local SEO</strong></td><td><strong>50%</strong></td><td>Profile fields, photos, reviews and rating — the <Link href="/completeness-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">profile</Link> side of the audit</td></tr>
              <tr><td><strong>AEO</strong> (answer engines)</td><td><strong>25%</strong></td><td>Whether your own website answers a customer&apos;s question clearly: title, description, phone, services, hours</td></tr>
              <tr><td><strong>GEO</strong> (generative engines)</td><td><strong>25%</strong></td><td>Whether AI crawlers can read your site, and whether structured data describes your business</td></tr>
            </tbody>
          </table>
        </div>

        <p>Your overall score is the weighted average of those three — but only of the ones we could actually measure. If your business has no website, AEO and GEO are marked <em>not measurable</em> and the overall is renormalised over Local SEO alone, so a missing site never silently caps your score.</p>

        <p>For example: Local SEO 72 (50%), AEO 45 (25%), GEO 30 (25%) contributes 36 + 11.25 + 7.5 = <strong>55/100</strong> overall. The Local SEO half alone accounts for 36 of those points, which is why profile work almost always moves the total fastest.</p>

        <p>Two things are deliberately <strong>not</strong> scored: Google Posts, Q&amp;A, owner replies and profile attributes. Google&apos;s public API does not expose them, so they appear in your report as <em>check manually</em> with no weight attached. Fix them because they help customers — not because they move a number we cannot verify.</p>

        <h2>Step 2: Fix in the Right Order</h2>

        <SeoDiagram caption="Not all fixes take the same time. Here's the order that produces the fastest visible improvement.">
          <PriorityFlowDiagram />
        </SeoDiagram>

        <p>Your report already sorts this for you: red items first, amber next, and the <em>check manually</em> list last. Here is why that order is what it is:</p>

        <ol>
          <li>
            <strong>Profile fields first.</strong> Name, address, phone, website, hours, description and category are entirely within your control — no customer behavior required — and most gaps can be fixed in under 20 minutes.{' '}
            <Link href="/completeness-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">See the Completeness Score Guide →</Link>
          </li>
          <li>
            <strong>Photos second.</strong> Uploading a batch of real job photos takes an afternoon, not weeks, and shows up on your very next audit run. Google&apos;s public API exposes a maximum of 10 photos, so the report compares you on that basis only.{' '}
            <Link href="/visual-content-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">See the Visual Content Score Guide →</Link>
          </li>
          <li>
            <strong>Your website&apos;s structured data third.</strong> Adding LocalBusiness / Plumber markup with your name, phone, address and opening hours is a one-time job that lifts both AEO and GEO at once, because the same markup answers machines and humans.
          </li>
          <li>
            <strong>AI crawler access fourth.</strong> If your robots.txt blocks GPTBot or PerplexityBot, no amount of clean markup helps — the report checks this directly by fetching your robots.txt and llms.txt.
          </li>
          <li>
            <strong>Reviews — start immediately, expect the slowest movement.</strong> Unlike everything above, this depends on real customers acting, not just you updating a field. It sits inside Local SEO, the 50%-weighted score, so it is worth starting on day one.{' '}
            <Link href="/reviews-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">See the Reviews Score Guide →</Link>
          </li>
        </ol>

        <CalloutBox type="best-practice" title="Start Reviews on Day One">
          The practical takeaway: <strong>start reviews on day one</strong>, since it&apos;s the slowest-moving signal, while knocking out the profile fields, photos and structured data in the same week.
        </CalloutBox>

        <h2>Step 4: Re-Run the Audit</h2>

        <p>Run <Link href="/gmb-audit-tool" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">the audit</Link> again after your first week of fixes to confirm the fast-moving checks (profile fields, photos, robots.txt, structured data) have registered, then again at the one-month mark to see reviews and rank catch up. Treat this as a recurring monthly habit rather than a one-time check — profiles drift back toward incomplete and inactive without ongoing maintenance, which is exactly what the <Link href="/seo-for-plumbers#section-19" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Neerzy WhatsApp workflow</Link> is built to prevent going forward.</p>
