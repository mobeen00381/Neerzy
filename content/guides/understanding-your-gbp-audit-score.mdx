<p>If you&apos;ve just run <Link href="/gmb-audit-tool" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Neerzy&apos;s free Google Business Profile audit</Link> and landed on a number out of 100, this page answers the question that number raises: <strong>what does this actually mean, and what do I do about it?</strong></p>
        <p>Your report has three scores, not five. This page explains what each one measures, what is deliberately left out, and the concrete fixes that move each one.</p>

        <h2>How the Score Is Built</h2>

        <SeoDiagram caption="Three scores make up your overall result: Local SEO (50%), AEO (25%) and GEO (25%).">

        </SeoDiagram>

        <div className="overflow-x-auto">

              <tr><td><strong>Local SEO</strong></td><td><strong>50%</strong></td><td>Profile fields, photos, reviews and rating, name/address/phone consistency</td></tr>
              <tr><td><strong>AEO</strong> — answer engines</td><td><strong>25%</strong></td><td>Whether your homepage answers a customer&apos;s question: title, description, phone, trade, hours</td></tr>
              <tr><td><strong>GEO</strong> — generative engines</td><td><strong>25%</strong></td><td>AI crawler access (robots.txt, llms.txt) and structured data describing your business</td></tr>
            </tbody>
          </table>
        </div>

        <p>Each score is out of 100, measured over <em>verified</em> checks only. Your overall score is the weighted average of the scores we could actually measure: if you have no website, AEO and GEO come back <em>not measurable</em> and the overall is renormalised over Local SEO alone. A surface we cannot read never silently drags your total down.</p>

        <p>The same rule applies to individual checks. Anything that lives only inside your Google dashboard — posts, Q&amp;A, owner replies, attributes, photo albums past the 10 Google publishes — appears in your report as <em>check manually</em> with zero weight. You will never be asked to fix something we could not see.</p>

        <p>Example: Local SEO 72 (50%), AEO 45 (25%), GEO 30 (25%) gives 36 + 11.25 + 7.5 = <strong>55/100</strong> overall.</p>

        <p>Four things sit inside this score, each checked against Google&apos;s public data:</p>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Profile fields</strong> — name, address, phone, website, hours, description and primary category, each checked as present or missing. <Link href="/completeness-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Completeness guide →</Link></li>
          <li><strong>Reviews and rating</strong> — your total count against your trade&apos;s benchmark, your star rating, and whether Google shows a review from the last 12 months. <Link href="/reviews-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Reviews guide →</Link></li>
          <li><strong>Photos</strong> — how many Google exposes publicly (capped at 10) against your trade&apos;s target. <Link href="/visual-content-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Visual content guide →</Link></li>
          <li><strong>Consistency</strong> — whether your trade word appears in your business name, and whether the phone number on your own website matches the one on your profile end to end. <Link href="/local-seo-optimization-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Local SEO guide →</Link></li>
        </ul>

        <CalloutBox type="important" title="Why It Carries Half the Weight">
          This is the surface where customers actually find you: the map pack and the profile page they land on before they call. Every check here is read from the same public data Google itself uses.
        </CalloutBox>

        <hr />

        <h2>AEO — 25%</h2>

        <p>AEO (answer-engine optimisation) is about whether a machine can answer <em>&quot;who is a good plumber near me?&quot;</em> using your own website. The audit fetches your homepage and checks five things: a title that names you or your trade, a meta description that says what you do and where, your phone number appearing on the page, your trade word appearing in the copy, and your opening hours being stated.</p>

        <ul>
          <li><strong>Title and description</strong> are the two lines assistants and search results quote most often.</li>
          <li><strong>Phone on the page</strong> matters because a caller needs the number without clicking through to a contact form.</li>
          <li><strong>Hours on the page</strong> answer the second question every emergency customer asks.</li>
        </ul>

        <p>If your site cannot be fetched at all (a firewall, a 403, a dead domain), the AEO checks come back <em>not measurable</em> with the reason, rather than failing you for something a bot block caused. See <Link href="/ai-search-visibility-local-business" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">AI Search Visibility for Local Businesses</Link> for the wider picture.</p>

        <hr />

        <h2>GEO — 25%</h2>

        <p>GEO (generative-engine optimisation) asks a narrower question: if an AI assistant wanted to read your site and quote your business, <em>could it?</em> Two things decide that.</p>

        <ul>
          <li><strong>Crawler access.</strong> The audit reads your robots.txt and checks whether GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot or similar are blocked, and whether an llms.txt file exists. Blocking them is a hard stop: no amount of good copy gets past it.</li>
          <li><strong>Structured data.</strong> The audit reads your JSON-LD and checks for a LocalBusiness type (Plumber, Electrician, Dentist...), a telephone, a postal address, opening hours, plus FAQ and Service markup where relevant. This is the markup that lets a machine state your details instead of guessing.</li>
        </ul>

        <p>Both are one-time jobs that stay done, which makes GEO the cheapest score on this list to move.</p>

        <hr />

        <h2>What Is Not Scored</h2>

        <p>Google Posts, Q&amp;A, owner replies, attributes and photo albums past the public 10-photo cap are not scored anywhere in your report. Google does not expose them through the public API, so the honest thing is to list them as <em>check manually</em> and let you confirm them in your dashboard. The habits still matter — <Link href="/engagement-score-guide" className="text-[#0F5132] hover:underline">Engagement &amp; Activity guide</Link> explains why — they just do not move a number we cannot verify.</p>
