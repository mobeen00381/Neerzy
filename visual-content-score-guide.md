# How to Improve Your Visual Content Score

Visual Content is weighted at 20% in [Neerzy's free GBP audit](https://www.neerzy.com/gmb-audit-tool) — the third-highest category after Completeness and Reviews & Reputation. It's also one of the fastest to move, since it depends entirely on you uploading photos, not on customer behavior.

## 1. What This Score Measures

- **Total photo count**, benchmarked against 10+, 50+, and 100+ thresholds
- **Recency of uploads** — whether photos have been added in the last 30 days
- **Logo and cover photo quality**
- **Photo diversity** — interior, exterior, and team photos, not just one type repeated
- **Video content presence**

## 2. Why Google Cares

Photo volume and recency are among the hardest signals for an inactive or fake business to fake convincingly. A profile with fresh, varied, weekly photos is strong, low-cost evidence to Google that a real business is actively operating right now — which is exactly the kind of "is this business alive" signal the algorithm is trying to detect across all five categories, made unusually concrete here.

## 3. Common Reasons for a Low Score

- **Photos were uploaded once, when the profile was created, and never touched again** — the single most common pattern among plumbing businesses.
- **All photos are the same type** — usually just a logo and a truck wrap, with no actual job-site or team photos.
- **No video at all**, even though video is checked as its own sub-signal and most competitors haven't added any either.
- **Photo count sits in the 10–30 range**, clearing the lowest threshold but falling well short of the 50+ and 100+ benchmarks that score higher.

## 4. Real Examples

A plumbing company with 12 photos, no video, and no uploads in the last six months will typically score in the 20–40 range on this category — clearing the "has photos" bar but falling short on every other sub-metric. A business adding 4–6 fresh job photos a week, with a mix of completed work, team shots, and at least one video, will typically reach the 80–100 range within two to three months of consistent uploading.

## 5. Priority Checklist

- [ ] Upload photos from every job going forward, not just a batch once in a while
- [ ] Mix photo types: completed work, before-and-afters, your team, your trucks
- [ ] Add at least one short video if you currently have none
- [ ] Update your logo and cover photo if they're outdated, blurry, or missing entirely
- [ ] Aim for the 50-photo threshold first, then 100+ over time

## How This Fits Your Overall Audit Score

At 20% weight, Visual Content moves your overall score less than Completeness or Reviews individually, but it's often the fastest category to fix in bulk — a single afternoon spent uploading a real photo backlog can shift this category more in one sitting than any other category allows.

## Common Questions

**Do stock photos count toward my photo score?**
No — the audit is designed to reward real, business-specific photos, and stock or generic imagery doesn't build the same trust with actual customers browsing your profile either.

**How often do I need to upload new photos to keep this score up?**
Weekly is the realistic target for busy plumbing businesses — one or two photos from each completed job maintains both the count and the recency sub-metrics without requiring a dedicated photography session.

**Does photo quality matter, or just quantity?**
Both are checked separately — a large volume of blurry, poorly lit photos won't score as well as a smaller set of clear, well-composed ones, though quantity still matters on its own.

**Is video really necessary, or just a bonus?**
It's checked as its own sub-signal, so it does contribute — but it's a smaller factor than photo volume and recency, so prioritize consistent photo uploads first if you have to choose.

## 6. Common Mistakes

- **Uploading a large batch once and then stopping** — recency is scored separately from total count, so a stale backlog doesn't fully substitute for ongoing activity.
- **Only uploading exterior or logo shots** — diversity is a specific sub-check, and a profile heavy on one photo type scores lower than a varied one.

## 7. When to Re-Run the Audit

Photo-based improvements are among the fastest to register — re-run the audit within a day or two of a significant upload batch to see the count and recency sub-scores move.
